Academic research.

Undergraduate thesis · University of Cyprus

Energy-Aware Scheduling for Retrieval-Augmented Generation Systems

Research in progress

I’m studying how to run retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workloads more energy-efficiently with GPU-accelerated Qwen models served through vLLM.

On Linux and CloudLab GPU infrastructure, I profile energy use and performance for vector indexing and re-indexing with FAISS and HNSW. I’m investigating how to schedule workloads around energy availability and operational constraints.
